Reverse-Klondike Rules & Mechanics

Instead of standard card suits and face cards, Thumbstack uses a streamlined 30-tile system designed for fast, thumb-friendly sessions.

  • The Deck (30 Tiles) Three distinct color families — Coral ▲, Teal ◆, and Amber ● — with values 0 through 9 (10 tiles per color family).
  • Tableau Builds UP (0 → 9) Five columns grow upward from the bottom control row. Stack tiles in ascending numerical order onto the next higher value of an alternating color.
  • Foundations Build DOWN (9 → 0) Three dedicated color slots build down from 9 to 0 in exact matching colors. Placing a tile into a foundation awards points and keeps the board moving.
  • Fast Auto-Move & Cascades Double-tap or long-press any playable tile to immediately send it to its valid foundation or optimal column.
  • Auto-Finish & Glass Shatter Once stock and waste are cleared, remaining tiles automatically cascade home in rapid succession, culminating in an upward celebratory glass shatter spray.

Strategy & Highlights

  • Build UP in the tableau with alternating colors.
  • Build DOWN in foundations with matching colors.
  • Empty columns accept any single tile or valid run.
  • Smooth multi-tile dragging with tactile haptic snaps.
  • 100% native Swift 6 and Apple Liquid Glass design.
