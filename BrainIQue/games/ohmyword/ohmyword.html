Anagram Mechanics & Progression

Quick, satisfying rounds designed to challenge your word recall and reveal subtle linguistic connections.

  • Dynamic Round Targets Each round provides 14 to 90 valid subwords organized cleanly from shortest to longest.
  • Two Paths to Advance Clear the round by discovering 60% of all valid words, or immediately unlock the next round by solving the master 6-letter word.
  • Integrated Definitions Tap any discovered word to instantly view its official dictionary definition without leaving the game.
  • Smart Lexicon & Bonus Words Built-in curated wordlists recognize valid bonus words and award credit while maintaining strict profanity guard filters.
  • Tactile Liquid Glass Rack Shuffle letters, tap or drag to spell, and enjoy responsive haptic clicks on every submitted word.

Strategy & Highlights

  • Hunt for the full 6-letter word for instant round completion.
  • Tap any solved word to learn its definition.
  • Shuffle the rack frequently to see fresh anagram combinations.
  • Persistent round streaks and best scores sync via iCloud.
  • Playable on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
