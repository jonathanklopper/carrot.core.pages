Classic merges with a math twist

Mergemathica keeps the satisfying swipe-and-merge loop, then adds math-flavored modes and board sizes for quick runs that still leave room for better decisions.

  • Classic and expanded boards 4x4, 5x5, and 6x6 boards are available in the full game.
  • Undo history Undo, saved stats, Game Center achievements, and leaderboard support help every run count.
  • Math-flavored merge modes Play Tripled+, Squares, Primes, Fibonacci, and Factorials when you want a different kind of run.
  • Theme customization Personalize your boards and colors to match your style.
  • Local-first persistence Progress saved locally with no third-party analytics SDKs.
