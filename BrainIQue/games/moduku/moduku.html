Dimension-Shifting Deductive Logic

Moduku brings fresh tactical variety to classical number placement puzzles by pairing traditional rectangular boxes with irregular polyomino jigsaw shapes.

  • Multiple Grid Dimensions Choose from mini 4x4, irregular 5x5 jigsaw, standard 6x6, 7x7 heptomino jigsaw, 8x8, and classic 9x9 boards.
  • Jigsaw Polyominoes Irregular sub-regions bend across the grid with dynamic rotations and reflections, demanding spatial awareness alongside numerical deduction.
  • Pure Deductive Solvability The built-in solver verifies unique solvability using rigorous deduction techniques (naked singles, hidden singles, pointing reductions) with guaranteed zero guessing.
  • Geometry-Aware Keypad A floating popover keypad appears right at your finger, intelligently arranged to match the geometry of the selected region.
  • Pencil Notes & Error Checking Pencil in candidate numbers, erase with one tap, track mistakes, and keep stats synced across your devices via iCloud.

Strategy & Highlights

  • Every puzzle has exactly one logical solution.
  • Zero guessing required: pure deductive reasoning.
  • Use pencil notes mode for multi-candidate cells.
  • Irregular jigsaw regions create novel intersection clues.
  • Crafted with native Liquid Glass and responsive touch.
