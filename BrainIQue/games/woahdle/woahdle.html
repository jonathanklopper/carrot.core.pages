Word Guessing Mechanics & Features

Deduce hidden words with strategic letter choices, clear visual feedback, and smooth typing.

  • Multiple Word Lengths Choose your challenge level by solving 5-letter, 6-letter, or 7-letter secret words.
  • Adaptive Clue Feedback Color-coded board cells and keyboard keys indicate exact matches (Correct), misplaced letters (Present), and excluded characters (Absent).
  • Anti-Repetition Window A 20-word rolling exclusion window prevents recently seen target words from repeating, keeping runs fresh.
  • Hardware Keyboard Support Type naturally using on-screen Liquid Glass keys on iPhone or physical keyboards on iPad and Mac.
  • Persistent Streak Tracking Win rates, guess distribution histograms, and streak milestones sync across all devices via iCloud.

Strategy & Highlights

  • Start with vowel-heavy opener words.
  • 7-letter mode offers deeper anagram and suffix deductions.
  • Use physical keyboards on Mac and iPad for speed.
  • No daily wait timers: play as many rounds as you like.
