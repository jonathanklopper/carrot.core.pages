BrainIQue

Tactile Apple puzzle suite with Liquid Glass aesthetics

BrainIQue is an all-in-one puzzle and brain games app for Apple platforms. It brings together tactile, polished games into a single unified experience built on native SwiftUI, Swift 6 concurrency, fluid Liquid Glass aesthetics, and shared foundation services.

Games Included

BrainIQue currently ships with five distinct games designed for swift, tactile sessions on Apple platforms.

Mergemathica 2048 number and logic puzzle with classic and expanded boards (4x4, 5x5, 6x6), undo history, and math-flavored modes. Thumbstack Tactile reverse-Klondike solitaire played with 30 frosted glass tiles, alternating color cascades, and glass shatter finishes. Moduku Dimension-shifting Sudoku & polyomino jigsaw logic puzzle across 4x4, 5x5, 6x6, 7x7, 8x8, and 9x9 grids with zero guessing. Oh My Word! Fast-paced anagram rounds forming 3-to-6 letter words from scrambled letters with built-in dictionary lookup and round progression. Woahdle Tactical word guessing challenge across 5, 6, and 7-letter words with adaptive Liquid Glass keyboard feedback and persistent streaks.

Features & Design

  • Native Swift & SwiftUI Built from the ground up with Swift 6 concurrency, 100% native views, and optimized rendering.
  • Apple Liquid Glass UI Tactile, translucent materials, dynamic specular highlights, and fluid physical animations.
  • Unified Sensory Engine Cohesive haptic feedback patterns tuned for clicks, tile merges, and victory celebrations.
  • Local-First & Cloud Sync Your streaks, high scores, and save states sync seamlessly via iCloud with zero third-party servers.
  • Zero Ads or Tracking No account required, no ads, no trackers, and no third-party telemetry SDKs.
